# Hyjal and raid-selection assets

Downloaded 2026-09-09. These are source art and clean planning maps; no AI imagery
or raster retouching was used. The two portrait files were converted from their
source JPEGs to PNG and proportionally resized to fit within 256 pixels. They were
not cropped, so their original framing remains intact.

## Raid selection cards

- `maps/tactics/raid-black-temple.jpg` — 2400 × 1350 official Blizzard image of
  the Black Temple exterior. Source image: [Blizzard CDN](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/SJ1G9ARQR1BP1641592063488.jpg), published with [Burning Crusade Classic: Phase 3 is Now Live](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23764312/burning-crusade-classic-phase-3-is-now-live).
- `maps/tactics/raid-hyjal.jpg` — 4584 × 3056 artwork of Archimonde's assault on
  Nordrassil, clearly showing the World Tree and the battle. Source image:
  [Warcraft Wiki file](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/Archimonde_assaults_Nordrassil.jpg?7648d4), from the [Nordrassil entry](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nordrassil).

## Clean strategy maps

All three maps are clean, unannotated RaidPlan images at 1600 × 889.

- `maps/tactics/hyjal-alliance-map.jpg` — [Alliance Village](https://cdn.raidplan.io/raid/wow.hyjal/map/01.alliance.jpg).
- `maps/tactics/hyjal-horde-map.jpg` — [Horde Village](https://cdn.raidplan.io/raid/wow.hyjal/map/02.horde.jpg).
- `maps/tactics/hyjal-summit-map.jpg` — [Archimonde / summit](https://cdn.raidplan.io/raid/wow.hyjal/map/03.archimonde.jpg).

RaidPlan's current raid catalogue identifies these as the three Battle for Mount
Hyjal locations, with Kaz'rogal and Azgalor using the Horde Village map. Catalogue
source: [RaidPlan editor](https://raidplan.io/plan/create?raid=wow.tbc.hyjal).

## Boss portraits

- `maps/kazrogal-icon.png` — 256 × 173 proportionally resized PNG from the 850 ×
  575 in-game Kaz'rogal capture at [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/Kaz%27rogal.jpg?896482), associated with the [Kaz'rogal entry](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kaz%27rogal).
- `maps/azgalor-icon.png` — 243 × 256 proportionally resized PNG from the 708 ×
  746 in-game Azgalor capture at [Wowhead](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/86780-azgalor.jpg), associated with [Azgalor](https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/npc=17842/azgalor).
