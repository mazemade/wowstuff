Checked 2026-09-08 against the live [guild Black Temple workbook](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1FyVLgyE6RAgppltXd72VcGEi3OPGBhKn0iEwmD0U4yk/edit), `Boss Mother Shahraz` tab, including both embedded strategy images.

- `mother-source-door.png`: original door diagram; an optional formation.

- Spell icons: Icy Veins TBC icon assets, `inv_sword_53`, `spell_shadow_shadowmend`, `spell_shadow_impphaseshift`, and `inv_misc_gem_variety_02`.
- Mechanics cross-check: [Method](https://www.method.gg/guides/black-temple/mother-shahraz) and [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/tbc-classic/mother-shahraz-guide-strategy-abilities-loot).

The guild images explicitly require three tanks stacked for Saber Lash, ranged and healers beneath the statues (or the door corner), and three different escape directions for Fatal Attraction. The teleport can occur anywhere; the marked example is not a fixed spawn or safe spot. Raid healers cover affected players, with available healthstones and healing potions particularly useful for the runner farthest from healing coverage.

The preparation recommendation is 244 Shadow Resistance with buffs for non-tanks; tanks retain stamina, mitigation and avoidance gear. Engineering items are optional tools rather than required assignments. Do not infer equipped gear, available consumables or immunities from an imported name or class.

The sources disagree on some beam damage values and Fatal Attraction timing. The briefing teaches the beam responses without disputed damage numbers, and uses illustrative event timing. The 25-yard separation check and 18-yard silence radius drive the movement lessons. Enrage at 10% health is retained as a final tank-survival reminder. Prismatic Aura school pairings remain in the reference details.
