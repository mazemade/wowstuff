The guild source supplies the formation, Flame kill order, landing Misdirection and Lust plan. Three conventional tanks and a ranged Warlock are the default teaching composition. The guild's two-tank overlap is retained as a preparation note, not proof that anyone has suitable gear. Classes, imports and the diagram cannot verify resistance sets, crit immunity, avoidance or threat.

Corrections and interpretation:

- The sheet's tank-only Phase 3 Agonizing Flames note reflects an old PTR behavior. Teach raid spacing and long DoT recovery in phases 3 and 5.
- Dark Barrage is shown on one target. Omit disputed raw damage rather than copying the workbook's multiple-target claim or Method's apparent low-damage typo.
- Shear cannot miss. The preparation note excludes miss chance from the combined dodge/parry/block total; a blocking ability and the actual set still require verification.
- The blue Eye Blast impact must be avoided even with Fire Resistance. The source's suggestion to stand in ordinary fire during an adjustment is not taught as permission to cross a beam.
- Keep each Flame close to its glaive and the raid central. References disagree on the distant-player charge check; no exact raid charge radius is asserted. Drawn routes are schematic, not live range checks.
- Demon Form uses an isolated ranged tank: 20-yard Shadow Blast splash, 15-yard boss aura and 5-yard Flame Burst spacing. Four Shadow Demons paralyze their targets; allies must kill them before contact. Parasites are a separate pair of adds from the ground debuff.
- A Demon skip is a damage goal, never a guarantee. A partial transform can still require a Shadow Blast response. Ground/Demon handoffs and possible final-phase Demon repeats remain covered.
- Maiev's trap example includes activation and boss traversal. Trap placement varies; a distant unsafe trap calls for the guild's planned cooldown fallback. The scene does not promise a kill inside a cage.

All animation times, health changes, selected targets and coordinates illustrate actions. They are not encounter timers, damage estimates, live raid assignments or fixed safe lanes.
