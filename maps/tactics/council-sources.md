# Illidari Council briefing sources

Checked 2026-09-09 against a fresh export of the [guild Black Temple workbook](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1FyVLgyE6RAgppltXd72VcGEi3OPGBhKn0iEwmD0U4yk/edit), `Boss Illidari Council` tab, including its embedded positioning image. The guild tab is the primary source for the coordinated BoP mage pull, separate Malande assignment, Gathios/Veras movement, room-wide spread, dedicated poison healing, physical/magical interrupts, and optional mage ramp kite. Three conventional tanks plus a mage tank is the default; the sheet’s option to combine Gathios and Veras on a geared tank is retained as a reference reminder, not assigned automatically.

Friendly Blessing of Protection at the pull is distinct from Gathios’s blessings on Malande: no player Paladin is required for the boss to cast either immunity. Protection blocks physical interrupts; Spell Warding blocks magic interrupts. Reflective Shield does not itself prevent interrupts. Icy Veins and Wowhead agree on this despite the misleading historical shield tooltip. Do not teach Reliquary’s shield-removal mechanic here.

Zerevor’s stolen Dampen Magic is mandatory protection for the mage tank. Keep more than 10 yards away as supported by Icy Veins. Refresh only when the boss reapplies the stealable buff; the guild note says to cancel the old stolen buff immediately before the new steal. Do not assume a named player’s gear, talents, threat lead or PvP gloves. The mage tank is not the sole magical interrupt backup on Malande.

Sources differ on raw damage, healing totals and Vanish details. The briefing teaches shared health, heal interruption, poison followed by Envenom, and the tank pickup without disputed amounts. Times, target selection and route coordinates are teaching examples. Ground remains where it was cast; numbered destinations require an actual clear-ground check. The optional kite is schematic and does not promise removal of every AoE cast or simulate live threat. The combined example ends with continuing responsibilities, not an invented kill.
