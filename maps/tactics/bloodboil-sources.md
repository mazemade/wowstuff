The guild's three-group rotation and Phase 1 Bloodlust call drive the lessons. A successful
G1/G2/G3 rotation allows a 24-second Bloodboil debuff to expire before that group takes
another application 30 seconds later. Total applications are not simultaneous stacks.
Tank stack limits depend on survival and threat; class-specific numbers in the source image
are not hard safety thresholds. The Fel Rage stat prose in guides is inconsistent, so the
briefing emphasizes survival rather than reproducing the disputed damage modifier.
Sources also disagree on the exact scope of the end-of-rage threat reset. The operational
instruction is to prepare tanks, confirm control and then resume damage. Example movement,
health and mechanic sequencing are teaching aids, not live encounter predictions.
