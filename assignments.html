    How these invites are chosen & raid preparation

    Role shortages come first, followed by encounter jobs, missing buffs and support for your existing damage dealers. Each invite is considered with the others, and the completed lineup is checked for overlapping benefits. Assumes comparable gear and skill, with standard raid talents; this is composition advice, not a simulated DPS ranking.

    Bloodlust / Heroism is raid-wide on Anniversary. Extra shamans are considered for healing and party totems. Party buffs still need a suitable group layout.

    Invites are suggestions only. Import or add the actual players to update your roster and assignments. Class coverage does not confirm specialist gear, talents or a player's ability to do an encounter job.
